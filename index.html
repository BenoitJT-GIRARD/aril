aril

Identifiers under https://w3id.org/aril name the parts of a metadata vocabulary for animal behaviour data: the model, its shapes, its terms and its error codes.

They are frozen once minted. A term keeps its identifier through promotion and through deprecation, so a dataset that cites one stays readable.

Nothing is published under the namespace yet. This page exists so that it resolves while the first release is prepared.
